# Pricing Recommendation · Roamly Groups

## 1. Pricing goal

- **Goal:** Grow revenue from a known audience: existing users who already book in groups across separate accounts and drop off before paying, matching my Module 1 goal (Expand) and my Module 2 bet on milestone celebrations.

## 2. Pricing model

- **Model:** Transaction-based: Roamly earns its standard commission on every seat in a group booking, with no separate fee for using Groups.
- **Who pays, and for what:** Each guest pays their own share of the per-person price through a link; the organiser pays only their own place and any unpaid place they choose to cover; the honoured guest's share is split across the paying guests by default; hosts pay the standard commission on more seats.

## 3. Packaging

- **Tiers:** Good: Join a session, for groups of 6–12 happy to share a host's scheduled session, at the host's usual price (about £40–£70 a head); Better ★: Private session, for milestone celebrations of 6–12 who want the host to themselves, about 25% above the usual price (about £55–£90 a head); Best: Private celebration, a private session built around the occasion (a toast, photos, a menu or route the group chooses) plus one free date change, for the biggest milestones (about £110–£150 a head).

## 4. Assumption to test

- **The one assumption I would test before launch:** Guests at a milestone celebration will pay about 25% more a head for a private session, so most organisers choose Private session over joining a scheduled one.
