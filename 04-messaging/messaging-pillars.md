# Messaging Pillars · Roamly Groups

## 1. Fro/To market shift

- **From, life before:** One person carries the celebration: they choose, book and pay for everyone, then spend weeks chasing friends for their share.
- **To, life after:** The group carries it together: everyone commits and pays their own share up front, so the organiser picks something special and enjoys the day like everyone else.

## 2. Audiences

- **Number of audiences that need their own message:** Two: organisers and guests.
- **Why you picked this number:** Both meet Roamly Groups inside the product, the organiser when booking and the guest at the invite and payment page, and guests need their own reason to pay through a link they didn't choose; hosts are reached through host ops' terms instead.

## 3. Messaging pillars

### Audience 1: Organisers of milestone celebrations
- **Value prop:** Pick something special for the big day and enjoy it with everyone else, without fronting the bill or chasing anyone.
- **Capabilities:** One booking for 6–12 people with a vetted local host who has opted in to groups; each guest pays their own share through a link by a deadline, and the honoured guest's share is split across the group unless the organiser covers it; at the deadline, the organiser decides whether to cover or release any unpaid place; everyone sees one shared plan; and a guarantee, shown before anyone pays, of what everyone gets back if the booking falls through.
- **Evidence:** Repeat booking at 3× the industry average and 12,000+ vetted hosts; group proof comes from the stage 1 beta: completion rate against the multi-account baseline and organisers' own stories (targets, not yet results).

### Audience 2: Guests invited to the celebration
- **Value prop:** Say yes, pay your share in a minute and just turn up, with no bank details to swap or IOUs to remember.
- **Capabilities:** A personal invite link showing the plan, the host and your share, including any split of the honoured guest's place; pay by card before the deadline; your place back if you drop out before the cut-off, or a free transfer after it; the shared plan on your phone; a light path to your own Roamly account afterwards.
- **Evidence:** The same vetted hosts; the guest-to-own-booking rate from the beta (a target, not yet a result).

## 4. Copy across three surfaces

- **Surface 1:** In-app notification, shown when the group size reaches 6 or a group checkout starts: "Booking for 6 or more? Book once for the whole group. Everyone pays their own share through a link, so you don't front the bill."
- **Surface 2:** Email subject line: "Book the big day, then enjoy it like everyone else", with the preview text "Everyone pays their own share. No fronting the bill, no chasing."
- **Surface 3:** Social post: "You picked the date for her 40th. You found something special. Then you spent weeks chasing eleven friends for their share. Roamly Groups sorts the last bit: book a private cooking class, food tour or other local experience for 6–12 people with a vetted host who's said yes to groups. Send everyone a link and each person pays their own share by the deadline. Anyone who hasn't paid? Your call: cover them or free up the place. Pick something special. Then enjoy it with everyone else. Hosted by locals from Roamly's 12,000+ vetted hosts."

### Blind read of V1

Two cold AI reads stood in for a classmate, and each model also read GPT-6's visual for contrast ([all reads](ai-replies/3-blind-read.md)).

- **Who it's for:** both readers found the organiser who ends up as the debt collector, but guessed hen and stag parties as readily as birthdays.
- **What it does:** both got the mechanism, and both read it as "a split-payment and commitment tool"; neither could say what they'd book.
- **What makes them trust it:** moderate at best; "vetted" isn't explained, and there's no rating, review, refund or cancellation promise.
- **V2:** the copy now names the occasion and the experience (section 4), and the V2 ad above shows the experience as a booking card, says what Roamly is and adds the guarantee. Module 5 has since set the drop-out terms, now in the guest pillar; the guarantee, if a booking falls through, becomes the trust line once its terms are set.
