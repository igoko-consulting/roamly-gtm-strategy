# Positioning Framework & Statement · Roamly Groups

## 1. Positioning framework

- **For (target audience):** Organisers of milestone celebrations (big birthdays, anniversaries, reunions) booking one standout experience for 6–12 people.
- **Who want / need:** To give everyone one experience they'll remember, without fronting the whole cost or chasing friends for their share.
- **We offer:** Roamly Groups lets one person book a curated local experience, such as a food tour or a cooking class, for a group of 6–12, with each guest paying their own share and everyone seeing the same plan.
- **Because of (value proposition):** The organiser books something memorable in minutes and then enjoys the day with everyone else, without fronting the cost or chasing anyone.
- **Despite (barriers):** Not every Roamly host takes groups of 6–12, so the experience the organiser wants may not be bookable for their group on their date.
- **Better than (differentiation):** Paying on one card and chasing friends, or booking on Airbnb Experiences or GetYourGuide where one person still pays for everyone: Roamly pairs vetted local hosts who have opted in to groups with each guest paying their own share.

Updated after the pressure tests: "We offer", "Because of" and "Better than" now match the final statement. The [generation prompt](../PROMPTS.md#1-generation) used the first version.

## 2. Positioning statement

### First draft
Roamly Groups lets one person book a curated local experience, such as a food tour or cooking class, for up to 12 people, with each guest paying their own share and everyone seeing the same plan. For anyone organising a big birthday, anniversary or reunion, that means booking something memorable in minutes, then enjoying it as a guest instead of fronting the bill or chasing friends to pay.

Claude's statement, chosen over GPT-6's because it names the occasion and the outcome. Both, word for word: [generation](ai-replies/1-generation.md).

### Pressure test
- **Test I ran:** B · Competitor discomfort, against Airbnb Experiences in place of my Module 2 competitor, GoHen, which doesn't serve milestone celebrations.
- **What it revealed:** Airbnb could use it almost word for word: 'curated local experience', the examples and the shared plan are category defaults, and per-guest payment is a feature it could copy; only hosts who opt in to groups and a clear promise on unpaid seats are ownable.

The final statement started as Claude's rewrite from this test, with "booking something memorable in minutes" cut because both models called it generic. Tests C and A, run afterwards in place of the blind read, then made the rule on unpaid places plain and replaced "chef's table". Both models' replies, word for word: [test B](ai-replies/2-pressure-test.md) · [test C](ai-replies/3-stranger-clarity.md) · [test A](ai-replies/4-audience-recognition.md).

### Final statement
Roamly Groups lets one person book an experience with a vetted local host who welcomes groups, such as a private cooking class or food tour, for 6–12 people, with each guest paying their own share. For anyone organising a big birthday, anniversary or reunion, that means enjoying the day with everyone else instead of fronting the bill, and if someone hasn't paid by the deadline, it's your call whether to cover them or free up their place.

### Further tests
- **C · Stranger clarity:** both models, as strangers, named the product, the person and the benefit in their own words; both stumbled on the earlier closing clause, a double negative about unpaid seats.
- **A · Audience recognition:** GPT-6 recognised itself; Claude's persona only partly, and found the honoured guest: the birthday person who doesn't pay, which per-guest payment doesn't yet cover.
