# Positioning Framework & Statement · Roamly Groups

## 1. Positioning framework

- **For (target audience):** Organisers of milestone celebrations (big birthdays, anniversaries, reunions) booking one standout experience for 6–12 people.
- **Who want / need:** To give everyone one experience they'll remember, without fronting the whole cost or chasing friends for their share.
- **We offer:** Roamly Groups lets one person book a curated local experience, such as a food tour or a cooking class, for a group of up to 12, with each guest paying their own share and everyone seeing the same plan.
- **Because of (value proposition):** The organiser books something memorable in minutes and then gets to enjoy it as a guest, without fronting the cost or chasing anyone.
- **Despite (barriers):** Not every Roamly host takes groups of 6–12, so the experience the organiser wants may not be bookable for their group on their date.
- **Better than (differentiation):** Paying on one card and chasing friends, or booking on Airbnb Experiences or GetYourGuide where one person still pays for everyone: Roamly pairs vetted local hosts with each guest paying their own share.

## 2. Positioning statement

### First draft
Roamly Groups lets one person book a curated local experience, such as a food tour or cooking class, for up to 12 people, with each guest paying their own share and everyone seeing the same plan. For anyone organising a big birthday, anniversary or reunion, that means booking something memorable in minutes, then enjoying it as a guest instead of fronting the bill or chasing friends to pay.

- **What it revealed:** Airbnb could use it almost word for word: 'curated local experience', the examples and the shared plan are category defaults, and per-guest payment is a feature it could copy; only hosts who opt in to groups and a clear promise on unpaid seats are ownable.

The final statement is Claude's rewrite with "booking something memorable in minutes" cut, because both models called it generic. Both replies, word for word: [pressure test](ai-replies/2-pressure-test.md).

### Final statement
Roamly Groups lets one person book an experience with a vetted local host who welcomes groups, such as a chef's table or a private food tour, for 6–12 people, with each guest paying their own share by a set deadline. For anyone organising a big birthday, anniversary or reunion, that means enjoying the day as a guest, never covering a seat you didn't choose to.
